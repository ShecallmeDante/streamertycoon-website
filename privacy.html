Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Streamer Tycoon handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Streamer Tycoon does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, address, phone number, contacts, or email address through the game.

Streamer Tycoon uses third-party services that may automatically collect or process certain information. This may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location derived from an IP address, interactions with the game and advertisements, and diagnostic or performance information.

Advertising

Streamer Tycoon uses Google Mobile Ads to provide advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. Players may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward.

Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, interactions with advertisements, diagnostic information, and other information necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Where required by applicable law, consent choices regarding advertising and data processing may be presented before personalized advertising is used.

Third-Party Services

Streamer Tycoon uses services provided by Google, including Google Mobile Ads.

Information processed by third-party providers is subject to their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

You can learn more about how Google handles information here: Google Privacy Policy .

How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party services may be used to:

  • provide and display advertisements;
  • provide rewarded advertisement functionality;
  • measure advertising performance;
  • maintain and improve service performance;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues.

Game Progress and Local Storage

Streamer Tycoon stores game progress, game settings, and preferences locally on the user's device.

Streamer Tycoon does not currently require a player account and does not maintain a separate developer-operated online database of player accounts.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the operation of Streamer Tycoon. Third-party services used by the game may also apply their own technical and organizational security measures.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings stored locally remain on the user's device until they are cleared by the user, removed through the operating system, or the application is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the retention policies of those service providers.

Because Streamer Tycoon does not currently provide user accounts, there is no Streamer Tycoon online player account that needs to be deleted.

Children's Privacy

Streamer Tycoon does not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the game. Advertising and data processing are handled in accordance with applicable laws, platform requirements, and the configuration of the advertising services used by the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
